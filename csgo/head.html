If you want to create your own post-match threads:

Message /u/Undercover-Cactus on Reddit to join the Post-Match Team. It's very low committment. You'd only have to do whatever amount of work you want to do, and it's barely a time commitment to make a post if you're already watching the match. We let most people join, as we're always happy for more help. We prefer if people join the group so that we can coordinate who posts, rather than having multiple people trying to make a post as fast as possible for each big match. If you are not a part of the group, please wait at least 10 minutes after the match ends, after which you're welcome to make your own post as it's likely nobody in the group was available for that specific match.
